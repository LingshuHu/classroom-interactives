06 / Why activation functions matter

Without nonlinearity, stacking layers adds no new shape

Change the neuron's input z and compare identity, sigmoid, and ReLU—the functions in your lecture.

Current transformation

Output φ(z)
Hidden layers need nonlinear activations. If every hidden layer is just a weighted sum, those layers collapse mathematically into one weighted sum. Nonlinearity allows more flexible patterns.

Identity: φ(z)=z · Sigmoid: φ(z)=1/(1+e−z) · ReLU: φ(z)=max(0,z)
