What exactly is X?
Each input word is represented by a one-hot vector (one 1, otherwise 0). The hidden layer transforms it into a learned dense embedding. For CBOW, the input vectors are averaged; for Skip-gram, the center word is the input.

How the Word2Vec neural network works

One-hot inputs activate hidden neurons through their connection weights. Those weights are the word embeddings.

In this simplified Word2Vec model, the hidden layer has three linear neurons and no hidden-layer bias. Each word's E row is its stored embedding.

Word embeddings vs. hidden-neuron outputs

Embedding of one word = its connection weights in E. For CBOW, the hidden neurons output the average of the context words' embedding weights.
